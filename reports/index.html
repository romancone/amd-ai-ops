4. Image quality measurement

Each profile answers the same four image questions at 16K context: identify the Great Wall, locate Singapore landmarks, explain a Titanic scene and describe pizza ingredients.

Luna-6 (gpt-6-luna) scores saved answers against the images and fixed reference answers, with model names and settings hidden. The reference answers define 100% for this comparison.

  • Factual accuracy: 40 points
  • Visible details and relationships: 30 points
  • Completion of the image task: 20 points
  • Clear, concise wording: 10 points

The displayed quality is the mean of the four image scores. MTP off and on are scored separately. Keyword coverage does not count toward quality; failed image requests remain unscored. These four tasks do not measure overall model accuracy or long-context image quality.
