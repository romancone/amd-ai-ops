For Gemma 12B, HIP saves 18 ms prefilling 512 tokens; Vulkan saves 145 ms generating 128 tokens. Estimated combined text time is 2.831 / 2.703 s (HIP/Vulkan). E4B: 1.524 / 1.336 s; Qwen/Q4: 2.275 / 2.069 s. These estimates come from pp512/tg128 rates, not image latency.

Gemma 12B — text and images

Use HIP, f16/f16 and FA on. HIP retained a landmark Vulkan omitted; that explains the 1.25-point quality difference. Average image response time was 3.16 s on both, with different answer lengths. Choose Vulkan for faster text generation.

E4B — faster text, weaker images

Use HIP, f16/f16 and FA on; Vulkan is the faster generation alternative. Both missed the Titanic identification. Prefer Gemma 12B or Qwen when image quality matters.

Qwen — image quality or lower memory

Use Vulkan, f16/f16 and FA on for image quality. Q4/Q4 saves about 0.36 GiB in the recorded snapshots, with a slightly lower score and generation rate. HIP/Q4 matched Vulkan/Q4 quality but was slower. Qwen/f16's 0.25-point lead over Gemma/HIP is too small to establish a quality winner on four images.
