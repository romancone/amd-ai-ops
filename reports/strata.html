What happens when you switch between chats?

    A single slot served one request at a time as the client switched histories. The first two runs kept the same host RAM limits (50G high, 56G max) to compare cache sizes. At 1 GiB, the cache had too little room for B. At 2 GiB, Strata was 743 MiB short of the free RAM it required to save B.
